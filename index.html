About

From code to the core network

I'm a Computer Science graduate from Mohammad Ali Jinnah University (CGPA 3.46 / 4.0, merit scholar for all four years) currently working as a Resident Engineer in the Network Operations Center at Wateen Telecom Limited, where I design, implement and maintain LAN/WAN systems and resolve network incidents quickly to keep downtime low.

Before that I spent 14 months as a SOC Analyst at NanoTechx, and earlier built product as a front-end intern at 360XpertSolutions, mentored React Native developers at Bytewise, and mentored contributors in Winter of Blockchain.

My academic work leans into machine learning and computer vision: a CNN-based non-invasive glucose monitor for Microsoft Imagine Cup, an attention UNet for low-light enhancement (27.82 dB PSNR), and a PyTorch re-implementation of PointNet. I keep studying NeuroAI and human visual processing in preparation for graduate study in data science and computational imaging.

Outside work I build communities: I founded the Microsoft Learn Student Community at MAJU, led Android at Google Developer Student Club, organized national conferences with Cisco and Microsoft communities, and write beginner guides on Git, cloud and AWS.
